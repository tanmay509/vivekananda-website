🏫 Our Training Center

Our Mission

At Vivekananda Computer Center, we believe technology education should be practical, affordable, and career-focused. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide by equipping students with skills that employers actually need.

Our History

Founded in 2010, VCC started as a small computer lab with just 10 computers. Today, we operate a state-of-the-art facility with modern labs, high-speed internet, and partnerships with leading tech companies for internships and placements.
